<p>Turn your flashlight on or off with two quick chopping motions. ChopTorch is a native Android app for Android 17 (API 37), with an English/French interface and system light/dark themes.</p>
<p>Enable detection yourself. A silent notification shows the foreground service and lets you stop it. Adjust sensitivity without changing the accepted gesture engine. Android may restore enabled detection after process loss, reboot (after first unlock) or app update. Explicit stop, force-stop, denied notifications and background restrictions are respected. There are no retry loops, maintenance jobs or permanent wake locks.</p>
<p>This is an initial beta. A running service does not guarantee sensor delivery during deep sleep. Screen-off reliability, battery overhead and 72-hour endurance have not been measured. Non-wake-up accelerometers cannot guarantee delivery while the processor is suspended.</p>
<p>No network permission, ads, analytics, account, audio or Google Play Services. Motion samples are processed in memory, never saved or exported. Only local sensitivity and activation intent are stored. The flashlight uses torch APIs, never a camera stream.</p>
<p>The public package is separate from older diagnostic previews. Stop detection in the older app before enabling this one; private settings are not migrated. Free software under GPL-3.0-or-later. Not affiliated with Motorola.</p>
