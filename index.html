Use

Open ChopTorch, enable Detect gestures and grant notification permission when prompted. Fresh installs start off. Hold the phone securely and make two quick chopping motions. Adjust sensitivity for lighter or firmer movements; stop detection in the app or its notification.

Background availability

Background gestures depend on device battery management and sensor behaviour during deep sleep; continuous availability is not guaranteed.

Privacy and license

Offline, with no ads, tracking or accounts. Motion data is processed in memory; settings stay on your device. No Internet or camera permission is required. GPL-3.0-or-later, without warranty.
